About AnxietyFlow

AnxietyFlow is an evidence-informed anxiety management website designed to organize coping methods by priority, category, safety, and practical usefulness.

The app is not a replacement for professional diagnosis, psychotherapy, emergency care, or medication management. It is intended as a structured self-support tool for education, reflection, and day-to-day coping.

Created by Guy Greenberg, an independent learner focused on psychology, technology, fitness, and practical mental health tools.
